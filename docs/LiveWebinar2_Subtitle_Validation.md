# Live Webinar 2 Subtitle Validation

## Caption Track

The `/live-webinar2` video now includes a default English `captions` track. The timed content was prepared from the supplied speaking-event video's speech transcription and formatted as WebVTT.

## Browser Verification

On September 8, 2026, the browser verified a loaded captions track with `readyState: 2`, `trackMode: showing`, and 12 timed cues. The track was enabled by default and the first cue reads: “My name is Dr. Jumana Al-Deek.”

At 00:25, the active caption was: “A lot of women—an entire generation of women—have been scared off hormone therapy because of this one.” The video remained operational while captions were active.

The captions are presented through the native video controls, allowing visitors to turn them off or back on as needed.
