## Top 5 Articles to Write First

### Recommended Writing Order

| Priority | Title | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Why Does Weight Gain Feel Different in Menopause? | Highest search volume, most universal entry point for the audience, validates the core pain point that drives every other service |
| **2** | What Is Food Noise and Why Is It So Common in Midlife? | Explosive search growth, bridges empathy and GLP-1 education, one of the highest-converting topics for consultation bookings |
| **3** | Semaglutide vs. Tirzepatide: What Women in Midlife Should Know | High commercial intent, patients are actively comparing before booking — being the best resource on this topic captures decision-ready leads |
| **4** | Can Hormone Therapy Help With Weight, Sleep, and Metabolism? | Opens the HRT conversation beyond hot flashes, introduces a service most women don't know they need, strong trust-builder |
| **5** | You've Been Putting Yourself Last for Years. Here's Why That Has to Change. | The permission-giving article — emotionally resonant, highly shareable on social media, and directly mirrors the exit-intent dialog language already on the site |

### Why This Order

Articles 1–3 target the highest-volume searches and capture women who are actively researching — they are in the "awareness and consideration" phase and need information before they will book. Article 4 introduces a second service line (hormones) to women who came in through the weight loss door, expanding the potential value of each visitor. Article 5 is the emotional anchor — it speaks to the identity of the audience, converts readers who are intellectually interested but emotionally hesitant, and is the most likely to be shared on social media, driving organic reach from day one.
