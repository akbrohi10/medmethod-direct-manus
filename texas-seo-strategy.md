# Texas SEO Strategy — MedMethod Direct

## Market Overview

Texas is the second-largest U.S. state by population (30.5M+) with massive suburban growth corridors around Houston, Dallas-Fort Worth, Austin, and San Antonio. The state has a strong telehealth-friendly regulatory environment and a large concentration of women 35–65 in affluent suburban communities — MedMethod's primary demographic.

Key competitors in the Texas telehealth menopause/GLP-1 space include Winona (national telehealth HRT), Midi Health (menopause-focused virtual care), Texas Medical Weight Loss Clinic (state-specific GLP-1 telehealth), and CHRONOS BHW (virtual weight loss). Local in-person competitors are fragmented — mostly single-provider OB/GYN practices and med spas.
