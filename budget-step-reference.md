# Budget Step Reference Design Notes

From the screenshot:
- Title: "Choose your care team"
- Subtitle: "Your membership includes ongoing support, accountability, and expert guidance. Medication is billed separately — no charge until approved."
- Term toggle: 3 Months | 6 Months (selected/dark) | 12 Months (with "best" badge)
- Cards stacked vertically with:
  - Tier label (TIER 01, etc) or badge (★ Most Popular)
  - Plan name (bold, large)
  - Price: $X/mo (right-aligned)
  - Tagline below name
  - Billed total: "$X billed · 6-mo term" + "+ $X initiation" in green/accent
  - "See details ∨" expandable
  - Featured cards (Transform, Longevity) have program images on right side
- Card styling: rounded borders, dark bg on featured cards (Transform has dark navy, Longevity has dark olive/gold)
- Bottom: "Select Plan & Continue" button (full-width, rounded)
- Trust badges: "HIPAA Compliant" + "256-bit Encrypted"
