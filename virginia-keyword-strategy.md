## Part 1: Virginia Keyword Map

### The Competitive Landscape

Northern Virginia has a well-established in-person women's health market. The key competitors occupying local search results include:

- **Tysons Gynecology / The Menopause Center** (Vienna, VA) — the most aggressive local SEO player, with dedicated pages for McLean, Reston, Great Falls, and Fairfax
- **Women's Global Health of Northern Virginia** (McLean) — targets "menopause specialist McLean VA"
- **Annandale OB-GYN** — targets "best menopause doctor near McLean Virginia"
- **Capital Women's Care** (Fairfax) — targets Fairfax broadly
- **Impressions MediSpa** (Fairfax) — targets "female hormone therapy Fairfax VA," serves McLean, Tysons, Reston, Great Falls, Arlington
- **Meno-Start** (McLean) — niche menopause-only clinic, McLean-focused
- **Proactiv Wellness Centers** — BHRT focus, Northern Virginia broad
- **Telehealth competitors**: Winona, Evernow, Gennev, Midi Health, Mochi Health (GLP-1)

**Key insight:** Most local competitors are in-person practices. The telehealth competitors (Winona, Evernow, Gennev) target Virginia at the state level but have NO neighborhood-specific pages. This is the gap MedMethod Direct can own.

### "Near Me" Keyword Strategy

"Near me" searches are dominated by Google Maps local pack results (requires Google Business Profile). However, these keyword patterns still appear in standard search results and should be incorporated into page copy:

- *"menopause doctor near me"* — use in H1/H2 with location modifier
- *"hormone therapy near me"* — use in FAQ section
- *"GLP-1 near me"* / *"semaglutide near me"* — explosive growth, use in page body
- *"women's health clinic near me"* — use in meta description

**Recommended approach:** Each location page should include a section titled something like *"Looking for a Menopause Doctor Near [City]?"* that directly addresses the "near me" intent and pivots to the virtual advantage.
