Sources & how this is benchmarked

Every benchmark in this report comes only from credible, published research:

  • The score bands — converted from the Menopause Rating Scale, a validated clinical scale for rating menopause symptoms (Heinemann et al., 2004).
  • The “women your age” comparisons — SWAN, the Study of Women’s Health Across the Nation, which has followed 3,302 women through the menopause transition. Your sleep and hot-flash figures come from SWAN too.
  • Weight and waist changes — Greendale et al., 2019, from SWAN’s body-composition data.
  • Joint and muscle pain figures — Kruse et al., 2026, a study of 93,021 women.

This report is educational. It is not a medical diagnosis, and it does not replace care from your own clinician. Its bands are population severity categories, not clinical or treatment thresholds.
